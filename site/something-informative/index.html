Brewster's Tab / Brewster's Millions, 1985

Thirty million dollars.
Thirty days. One tab.

In Brewster's Millions, Monty Brewster has to spend $30 million in 30 days and own nothing at the end. I wanted to know how much he'd burned through at any minute of the movie.

It's a silly question with good numbers to crunch: a fixed total, a hard deadline and almost no prices. So every amount is an estimate with its working shown, and the rules of the will make a decent puzzle of their own.
